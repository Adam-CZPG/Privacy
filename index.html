Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

AIDQS built the Math Master: Brain Challenge app (package com.master.mathgame) as a free, ad-supported app. This SERVICE is provided by AIDQS at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. The app is designed for children and families.

In short: we do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as a name, e-mail address, photo or location. There are no accounts and no in-app purchases. Game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob that are set to be child-appropriate and non-personalized.

Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not ask users for any personal information. It has no sign-up, no chat, no user-generated content and does not access the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Game Progress Stored on Your Device

Your level progress, stars, badges, statistics and language choice are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, it shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a small banner on the home screen, a full-screen ad after some levels, and an optional video that the player can choose to watch to unlock the next level. Ads are never shown while a question is being answered.

Because the app is directed at children, we configure AdMob to:

  • treat every ad request as child-directed (COPPA) and for users under the age of consent (GDPR);
  • show only non-personalized ads (no interest-based advertising or remarketing);
  • limit ad content to the “G” (general audiences) rating.

To deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, Google may automatically collect limited technical information, such as the device's IP address, device model, operating system version and approximate (country-level) location derived from the IP address. For child-directed requests Google does not use the advertising ID for personalization. Please see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Other Third-Party Services

The app is distributed through Google Play and uses Google Play services, which may collect technical information (for example crash reports and device information) according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play services
  • Google AdMob

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only third-party SDK that receives data is Google AdMob, configured as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

We do not keep any user data on our servers. Progress saved on the device is removed when the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

Security

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data on our side that could be exposed. Communication between the app and Google's ad services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Cookies

The app does not use cookies itself. Google's advertising services may use similar device-level technologies as described in Google's policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes are posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. You are advised to review this page periodically.
