Privacy Policy – TryFixPass

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Akhmadjon Otajanovich Ortikov (sole trader, OSVČ, Czech Republic) built the TryFixPass app (package com.gbline.gbexam), a practice app for school entrance exams. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. The app is designed for children and their parents.

In short: there are no accounts, no e-mail sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Results and the child's first name stay on the device. Only if the child chooses to join the league, duels or a class, the app sends a made-up nickname (an animal and a number), the country, the language and weekly points to our server. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored only on your device

The child's first name (optional, used only to greet the child), chosen country, grade, language, test results, statistics and settings are saved in the app's own storage on the device. They are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage in the phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

League, classes and following friends (optional)

These features are off until the child turns them on. When turned on, the app creates a random player code and sends to our server:

  • a nickname picked from a list (an animal emoji and a number) – never the real name;
  • the country and language chosen in the app (shown as a flag);
  • the number of points earned each week;
  • classes the child created or joined (grade, letter, mascot), which players follow each other, and preset cheers sent between players (there is no free-text chat).

The server runs on Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). The child can leave the league at any time in the app; this deletes the player and all related data from the server.

Duels

A duel is shared as a link through a messenger the child or parent chooses (for example WhatsApp or SMS). The link contains the duel settings, the score, the chosen avatar and the first name entered in the app, so the friend can see who challenged them. The link goes directly from person to person; we do not store it on our server.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the full version was bought on this Google account. Before the Google Play payment sheet opens, the app asks a short parents' question so that a child cannot buy by accident.

Notifications

If allowed, the app shows local reminders for the daily plan. They are scheduled on the device; no data is sent anywhere.

Advertising and analytics

The app shows no ads and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not use the advertising ID.

Third-party services

  • Google Play and Google Play Billing – app distribution and payments;
  • Supabase – server for the optional league, classes and following (data listed above).

Children's privacy

The app is intended for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not ask for real names, e-mail addresses, photos, contacts or location, and children cannot write free text to other players. If you are a parent or guardian and want any data deleted, use "Leave the league" in the app or contact us and we will delete it.

How to delete your data – TryFixPass

Data on our server (only if the child joined the league): the nickname, country, language, weekly points, classes, follows and cheers.

  1. Open TryFixPass and go to the Friends tab (Kamarádi).
  2. At the bottom, tap "Delete me from the league" (Smazat mě z ligy) and confirm.
  3. All of the data above is deleted from the server immediately. Nothing is kept.

You can also e-mail akhmadjonortikov@gmail.com with the subject "TryFixPass data deletion"; we delete the data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

Data on the device (name, results, settings): clear the app's storage in the phone's settings or uninstall the app.

Purchases are kept by Google Play as part of your Google account; see Google's privacy controls.

Data retention

Device data stays until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. League data stays until the child leaves the league or asks us to delete it.

Security

All communication with the server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). The server keeps only the nickname data described above.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
